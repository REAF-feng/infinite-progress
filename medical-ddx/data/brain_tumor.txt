Brain Tumor

Overview:
Brain tumors are abnormal growths in the brain tissue, which can be primary or metastatic, benign or malignant.

Clinical Presentation:
Common Symptoms:
- Headaches
- Seizures
- Neurological deficits
- Cognitive changes
- Nausea and vomiting

Diagnosis:
Key Diagnostic Tests:
- MRI
- CT scan
- Biopsy
- PET scan
- Neurological examination

Treatment:
Management Approaches:
- Surgery
- Radiation therapy
- Chemotherapy
- Targeted therapy
- Supportive care

Key Points:
- Brain Tumor is an important medical condition requiring proper diagnosis and management
- Early recognition and appropriate treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes
- Regular follow-up and monitoring are essential for optimal care
- Patient education and compliance with treatment recommendations are crucial
- Multidisciplinary approach may be beneficial for comprehensive care
