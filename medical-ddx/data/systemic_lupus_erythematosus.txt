Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE)

Overview:
SLE is a chronic autoimmune disease that can affect multiple organ systems including skin, joints, kidneys, heart, and nervous system.

Clinical Presentation:
Common Symptoms:
- Malar rash
- Joint pain
- Fatigue
- Fever
- Photosensitivity
- Kidney involvement

Diagnosis:
Key Diagnostic Tests:
- ANA
- Anti-dsDNA
- Anti-Sm
- Complement levels
- Complete blood count
- Urinalysis

Treatment:
Management Approaches:
- Antimalarials
- Corticosteroids
- Immunosuppressants
- Biologics
- Supportive care

Key Points:
- Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE) is an important medical condition requiring proper diagnosis and management
- Early recognition and appropriate treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes
- Regular follow-up and monitoring are essential for optimal care
- Patient education and compliance with treatment recommendations are crucial
- Multidisciplinary approach may be beneficial for comprehensive care
