A tension-type headache is the most common type of primary headache. The pain can radiate from the neck, back, eyes, or other muscle groups in the body. Tension-headache pain is often described as a constant pressure, as if the head were being squeezed in a vise. The pain is typically mild to moderate and affects both sides of the head. It does not typically cause nausea or vomiting, and it is not worsened by physical activity.
