Lymphoma

Overview:
Lymphoma is a group of blood cancers that develop from lymphocytes, including Hodgkin and non-Hodgkin lymphoma.

Clinical Presentation:
Common Symptoms:
- Swollen lymph nodes
- Fever
- Night sweats
- Weight loss
- Fatigue

Diagnosis:
Key Diagnostic Tests:
- Lymph node biopsy
- CT/PET scans
- Bone marrow biopsy
- Flow cytometry
- Immunohistochemistry

Treatment:
Management Approaches:
- Chemotherapy
- Radiation therapy
- Immunotherapy
- Stem cell transplant
- Targeted therapy

Key Points:
- Lymphoma is an important medical condition requiring proper diagnosis and management
- Early recognition and appropriate treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes
- Regular follow-up and monitoring are essential for optimal care
- Patient education and compliance with treatment recommendations are crucial
- Multidisciplinary approach may be beneficial for comprehensive care
