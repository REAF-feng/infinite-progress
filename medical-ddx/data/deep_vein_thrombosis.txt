Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT)

Overview:
DVT is the formation of blood clots in deep veins, most commonly in the legs, which can lead to pulmonary embolism.

Clinical Presentation:
Common Symptoms:
- Leg pain
- Swelling
- Warmth
- Redness
- May be asymptomatic

Diagnosis:
Key Diagnostic Tests:
- D-dimer
- Duplex ultrasound
- CT venography
- Wells score
- Physical examination

Treatment:
Management Approaches:
- Anticoagulation
- Compression stockings
- Early mobilization
- IVC filter (selected cases)

Key Points:
- Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT) is an important medical condition requiring proper diagnosis and management
- Early recognition and appropriate treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes
- Regular follow-up and monitoring are essential for optimal care
- Patient education and compliance with treatment recommendations are crucial
- Multidisciplinary approach may be beneficial for comprehensive care
