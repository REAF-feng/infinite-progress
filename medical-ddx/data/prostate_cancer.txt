Prostate Cancer

Overview:
Prostate cancer is a malignant tumor of the prostate gland, most common in older men and often slow-growing.

Clinical Presentation:
Common Symptoms:
- Urinary symptoms
- Blood in urine
- Erectile dysfunction
- Bone pain
- Often asymptomatic

Diagnosis:
Key Diagnostic Tests:
- PSA
- Digital rectal exam
- Prostate biopsy
- MRI
- Bone scan

Treatment:
Management Approaches:
- Active surveillance
- Surgery
- Radiation therapy
- Hormone therapy
- Chemotherapy

Key Points:
- Prostate Cancer is an important medical condition requiring proper diagnosis and management
- Early recognition and appropriate treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes
- Regular follow-up and monitoring are essential for optimal care
- Patient education and compliance with treatment recommendations are crucial
- Multidisciplinary approach may be beneficial for comprehensive care
