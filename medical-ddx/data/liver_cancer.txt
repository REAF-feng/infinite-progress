Liver Cancer

Overview:
Primary liver cancer includes hepatocellular carcinoma and cholangiocarcinoma, often developing in patients with chronic liver disease.

Clinical Presentation:
Common Symptoms:
- Abdominal pain
- Weight loss
- Ascites
- Jaundice
- Fatigue

Diagnosis:
Key Diagnostic Tests:
- CT/MRI
- Alpha-fetoprotein
- Ultrasound
- Biopsy
- Liver function tests

Treatment:
Management Approaches:
- Surgery
- Liver transplant
- Ablation
- Chemoembolization
- Targeted therapy

Key Points:
- Liver Cancer is an important medical condition requiring proper diagnosis and management
- Early recognition and appropriate treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes
- Regular follow-up and monitoring are essential for optimal care
- Patient education and compliance with treatment recommendations are crucial
- Multidisciplinary approach may be beneficial for comprehensive care
