Celiac Disease

Overview:
Celiac disease is an autoimmune disorder triggered by gluten consumption, leading to small intestine damage and malabsorption.

Clinical Presentation:
Common Symptoms:
- Diarrhea
- Abdominal pain
- Weight loss
- Fatigue
- Dermatitis herpetiformis

Diagnosis:
Key Diagnostic Tests:
- Serology testing
- Small bowel biopsy
- Genetic testing
- Gluten challenge
- Response to gluten-free diet

Treatment:
Management Approaches:
- Strict gluten-free diet
- Nutritional supplementation
- Monitor for complications
- Dietary counseling
- Regular follow-up

Key Points:
- Celiac Disease is an important medical condition requiring proper diagnosis and management
- Early recognition and appropriate treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes
- Regular follow-up and monitoring are essential for optimal care
- Patient education and compliance with treatment recommendations are crucial
- Multidisciplinary approach may be beneficial for comprehensive care
