Testicular Cancer

Overview:
Testicular cancer is a malignancy arising from testicular tissue, most common solid tumor in young men aged 15-35.

Clinical Presentation:
Common Symptoms:
- Testicular lump
- Testicular pain
- Scrotal swelling
- Back pain
- Breast enlargement

Diagnosis:
Key Diagnostic Tests:
- Physical examination
- Testicular ultrasound
- Tumor markers
- CT scan
- Chest X-ray

Treatment:
Management Approaches:
- Orchiectomy
- Chemotherapy
- Radiation therapy
- Surveillance
- Retroperitoneal lymph node dissection

Key Points:
- Testicular Cancer is an important medical condition requiring proper diagnosis and management
- Early recognition and appropriate treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes
- Regular follow-up and monitoring are essential for optimal care
- Patient education and compliance with treatment recommendations are crucial
- Multidisciplinary approach may be beneficial for comprehensive care
