# 套磁邮件模板三版（欧洲岗位制博士联系专用）

> **使用说明**：三版模板适配不同联系场景与个人风格。核心原则：①200字以内正文 ②必须提到对方具体论文 ③附CV PDF ④7天无回复可跟进1次。所有`[方括号]`内为需要替换的个性化内容。

---

## 版本A：学术严谨型（最推荐｜回复率最高）

> **适用场景**：已对该导师课题组有深入研究，自己的背景与该方向高度匹配。适合发给你Excel表中匹配度评分≥4的导师。

```
Subject: PhD Inquiry – [Your Research Topic, max 8 words]

Dear Prof. [Last Name],

I am a [X]th-year Basic Medical Sciences undergraduate at Sichuan University's
West China School of Basic Medical Sciences (5-year Bachelor of Medicine).

Your recent study on [specific finding, e.g., "the role of TREM2+ macrophages
in glioblastoma immunosuppression"] (Nature Cancer, 2025) is directly relevant
to my current undergraduate research. In Prof. [Your Advisor]'s lab, I have
been investigating [your topic – 1 sentence], where I independently perform
[list 3–4 specific techniques, e.g., "multicolor flow cytometry, intracranial
xenograft models, and scRNA-seq analysis"].

I am writing to ask whether you anticipate PhD openings in your group for
[season, e.g., Fall 2027]. I am particularly interested in extending your
findings on [specific mechanism] to ask [your scientific question – 1 sentence].

My CV is attached. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my experimental
background could contribute to your group's research direction.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Email] | [WhatsApp]
[ORCID: XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX]
Attach: CV_YourName.pdf
```

**为什么这版回复率最高**：
- 第1句不废话，直接亮身份（5年制医学学士→德国PI立刻识别为中国医学本科）
- 引用对方具体论文+期刊+年份→证明你做过功课（80%的套磁邮件引用了论文但写错期刊名）
- 列出3–4个具体技术（而非"molecular biology techniques"这种空泛表述）→PI可以直接判断你能不能进组就干活
- 不超过200字→PI可以在手机上30秒读完

## 版本B：热情好奇型（适合年轻PI和态度开放的中型课题组）

> **适用场景**：导师年龄<45岁，课题组风格有活力，研究方向与你"部分匹配但想拓展"。适合第一次接触不太熟悉的领域。

```
Subject: Potential PhD candidate fascinated by your [specific approach, e.g., "organoid-based immunotherapy screening platform"]

Dear Prof. [Last Name],

Your group's work combining [technique A] with [technique B] to study
[disease/problem] really captured my attention — it strikes me as exactly the
kind of integrative approach that [field] needs right now.

I'm a [X]th-year medical undergraduate at Sichuan University (West China),
currently working on [your project – 1 sentence] under Prof. [Advisor].
My hands-on experience includes [2–3 key skills], and I'm actively learning
[1 new skill you're excited about] because I believe it will be essential
for [future direction].

I would love to explore whether there might be a PhD opening in your lab
where I could contribute to [specific aspect of their work]. I've attached
my CV and would be grateful for any guidance on timing or opportunities.

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
[Email] | [WhatsApp]
Attach: CV_YourName.pdf
```

**适用逻辑**：
- 年轻PI（<45岁）更看重"热情+学习能力"而非"已有的完美匹配"，这版语气偏积极主动而非过度正式
- "actively learning XX because I believe..."展示了你的科研判断力（不只是在学技术，而是理解了为什么学）
- 适合转方向的场景（比如你的课题是肿瘤免疫，但你想扩展到神经免疫交叉领域）

## 版本C：直奔主题型（最简洁｜适合批量投递时节省时间）

> **适用场景**：公开岗位已放出且明确写了"contact Prof. XX for informal inquiries"。
> **注意**：这版只适用于对已发布岗位的意向询问，不适用于"冷email"未发布岗位的导师。

```
Subject: PhD position – [Job Reference Number] – [Your Name]

Dear Prof. [Last Name],

I am writing regarding the PhD position "[Project Title]" (Ref: [XXXXX])
advertised on [EURAXESS / your lab website].

I am a final-year medical undergraduate at Sichuan University's West China
School of Basic Medical Sciences, with [X] years of hands-on research
experience in [your field]. My background aligns directly with this position:

• [Skill A] – [brief evidence, e.g., "2 years independent experience"]
• [Skill B] – [brief evidence, e.g., "used in undergraduate thesis project"]
• [Skill C] – [brief evidence, e.g., "presented at XX conference, 2025"]

I would welcome an informal discussion about this opportunity. My CV is
attached for your review.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Email] | [WhatsApp]
Attach: CV_YourName.pdf
```

**适用逻辑**：
- 已发布岗位的PI会收到50–200封申请——他们没时间读长邮件
- 用bullet points直接亮出自己的技能与岗位的匹配度（而不是让PI自己去你的CV里找）
- "informal discussion"降低了PI的心理门槛（不是"请给我面试机会"，而是"可以聊聊吗"）

## 三版对比速查表

| 维度 | 版本A：学术严谨 | 版本B：热情好奇 | 版本C：直奔主题 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **长度** | ~180字 | ~150字 | ~120字 |
| **语气** | 正式、自信 | 积极、开放 | 高效、直接 |
| **论文引用** | ✅ 必须引用具体论文 | ⚠️ 提研究方向即可 | ❌ 不需要（岗位已有描述） |
| **最适合** | 匹配度≥4的导师，首次冷联系 | 年轻PI，方向部分匹配 | 已发布公开岗位 |
| **预期回复率** | 15–25% | 10–20% | 30–50%（但仅限岗位询问） |
| **附件** | CV | CV | CV |

## 套磁通用规则（三版均须遵守）

1. **发送时间**：当地时间周二至周四上午8:00–10:00（PI最可能在这个时间段检查邮件）
2. **邮件标题必须有你的名字或研究方向关键词**：否则PI可能当垃圾邮件忽略
3. **正文永远不超过200字**：超过200字=大概率不会被读完
4. **CV只发PDF，文件名规范**：`CV_FirstNameLastName.pdf`（不要用什么"最终版""new"之类的词）
5. **不要群发CC**：每封邮件独立发送（PI能看到你是否群发）
6. **跟进规则**：7天后未回复→发1次跟进（模板见下方）；第二次跟进仍无回复→放弃该导师

### 跟进邮件（7天无回复后使用）

```
Subject: Gentle follow-up – [Original Subject]

Dear Prof. [Last Name],

I hope this message finds you well. I'm gently following up on my email from
[date] regarding potential PhD opportunities in your group.

I understand you are likely very busy. If you have a moment to let me know
whether you anticipate openings, I would greatly appreciate it.

Thank you again for your consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```
